\hypertarget{md__r_e_a_d_m_e_autotoc_md34}{}\doxysection{\texorpdfstring{Security Features}{Security Features}}\label{md__r_e_a_d_m_e_autotoc_md34}

\begin{DoxyEnumerate}
\item {\bfseries{Biometric auth}}:\+ SHA-\/256 fingerprint hash — raw biometric never leaves the Pi
\item {\bfseries{Duplicate prevention}}:\+ Double-\/checked in both Mongo\+DB AND blockchain chaincode
\item {\bfseries{Vote secrecy}}:\+ AES-\/256 encrypted votes on ledger — only EC can decrypt
\item {\bfseries{Session security}}:\+ JWT expires in 15 minutes; cleared after vote
\item {\bfseries{Rate limiting}}:\+ 10 auth attempts per 15 min per IP
\item {\bfseries{Timing-\/safe comparison}}:\+ {\ttfamily crypto.\+timing\+Safe\+Equal} prevents timing attacks
\item {\bfseries{TLS everywhere}}:\+ All Fabric peer communication uses TLS
\end{DoxyEnumerate}
